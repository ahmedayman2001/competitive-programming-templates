 ✅ If your problem includes:


Range sum

Range add/update

Nested range operations       

Counting frequencies in subarrays

✅ Then Prefix Sum / Difference Array is likely the right tool.



🚀 Tip for You
Always think:

“Can I avoid looping over the full range by using a smart precomputation?”

If yes → prefix sums or difference arrays are probably your best friends! 🤝
